instrument bench

The game's own sequencer instruments, played through this project's reader, key splits, pitch formula and mixer. Assets come from fixtures/, extracted from your own install; nothing leaves this machine.

1.20s off (faithful) 0.50

Play it. The computer keyboard is live: Z S X D C V G B H N J M is one octave with its sharps above, Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U is the octave above that, and the arrow keys shift octave. Click anywhere outside a control first so the keys reach the page.

What to listen for. “Every slot at its base note” plays each sample at ratio exactly 1.0 — they should sound like one instrument, and a timbre step between them means the splits or the pitch formula are wrong. The coloured bar under each key shows which slot it resolves to, so the key-split zones are visible while you play them. Every pitched sample carries a loop point in its smpl chunk and the mixer honours it, so a note's length follows the note, not the sample divided by the playback rate — drag note length and every pitch should sustain for the same time.

The control. Switching engine to “browser resampler” plays the identical sample at the identical pitch through the browser's own resampler. Clean there and dirty here means the fault is ours. That control is what proved the earlier distortion was the wrong source material — the 4-bit SFX-bank copies — rather than anything in this pipeline.
