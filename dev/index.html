piano_C2..C6 listening test

The first thing in this project that can be judged by ear. Everything runs in this tab — the bank is read from your disk and never uploaded.

Drop sfxbank_compressed.fsb here or click to pick it — it is in gamedata/audio/ of your own install
0.50

The control that matters. Switch engine to “browser resampler”. That plays the identical decoded sample at the identical pitch through the browser's own resampler instead of ours. If it sounds clean and ours does not, the fault is ours and this page just found it. If both sound the same, the character is in the asset — these are 4-bit IMA ADPCM at 22050 Hz, which is not transparent — and no interpolator will remove it.

What else to listen for. The five base notes should sound like one instrument: each plays its own sample at ratio exactly 1.0, so any timbre step between them means the key splits are wrong. Across the chromatic run, listen at the octave boundaries — a jump in brightness there is the splits; a note that sounds flat or sharp is the pitch formula, and would mean finetune is in semitones rather than cents. The interpolator selector is an A/B for open question 7.

⚠️ And a caveat about the comparison itself. Nothing has yet proved the sequencer plays these samples. We know piano_C2..C6 exist in sfxbank_compressed.fsb and are the right shape; the link from an instrument's SampleGuids to actual audio is open question 1. If the in-game piano sounds different in kind rather than in cleanliness, that is the likelier explanation.
