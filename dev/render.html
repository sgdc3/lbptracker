Play your LittleBigPlanet 3 music

Open one of your LittleBigPlanet 3 levels and hear the songs written in its music sequencer the way the game plays them — the same instruments, the same echo and reverb, note for note. Then save any of them as a WAV. Everything happens on your own computer: the level you open never leaves this tab, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Engine switches — every default below is what was measured from the game

Each of these is still an open question, which is the only reason it is a switch. The pan width has a measured value but an unread mechanism; the output clip is measured but may or may not engage, because our absolute level against the game's has never been checked; reverb and echo come off to compare a render against a dry recording of the game; the voice pool is the engine's 32, and uncapping it is how its effect gets measured. Settled laws are not here — they are just what you hear.
