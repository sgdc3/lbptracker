lbptracker — level render, in the browser

The same renderSequencer that dev/render-level.ts runs under Node, in a worker. No OfflineAudioContext, no AudioBufferSourceNode.playbackRate: every sample is resampled, filtered and mixed by this project's own arithmetic, and the browser is only asked to play back a finished 48 kHz buffer. The level dump is opened from your own disk, not served.

Open your sequencers.jsonl Click or drop it here. The file is read in this tab and never uploaded.
