A chip is as long as its note grid and chips may overlap in time, though the game’s composers rarely let them; the selected one is drawn on top.

    2 A MIDI file in — it becomes the song

    Open a .mid Click here, or drop a MIDI file onto this box. Anything a DAW writes will do.

    A file that came from here brings its instruments with it; one from a DAW has none, so pick what its parts should sound like, then take it as the song.

    Replaces what is open; unsaved changes are lost.
    What survives the trip, and what cannot

    The grid. A step is a sixteenth and a note can sit a third of one late, so the file is written at 480 ticks per quarter: a step is 120 ticks and a third of one is exactly 40. Nothing is rounded.

    The glides. A note in this sequencer is a chain of control points and the engine ramps between them. MIDI pitch bend holds its value until the next message, so the export resamples a glide onto the same third-of-a-step grid the records use, and the import folds the samples back to the points they came from.

    What MIDI has no room for rides in text meta events: the tempo and swing, the mixer’s channels and volumes, and per part its instrument, row, level, pan and sends. A file that goes out and comes back is the same song.

    What it cannot carry. An MPE zone has fifteen member channels and this engine has thirty-two voices, so a dense passage runs out; a note that has to share a channel loses its own glide, and the count is always shown. Key and scale are folded into the note numbers on the way out, so an import comes back chromatic, sounding the same.

    What this bench is for

    Every slot at its base note plays each sample at ratio exactly 1.0 — they should sound like one instrument, and a timbre step between them means the splits or the pitch formula are wrong. The coloured bar on each key shows which slot it resolves to.

    The control. Switching engine to “browser resampler” plays the identical sample at the identical pitch through the browser’s own resampler. Clean there and dirty here means the fault is ours.
