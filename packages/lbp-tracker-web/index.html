Play the game’s instruments

LittleBigPlanet 3’s own sequencer instruments, live, through this project’s reader, key splits, pitch formula, mixer and output stage. Play them with the mouse, the computer keyboard or a MIDI controller, and turn every voice and effect parameter while it sounds. Assets come from your own install and nothing leaves this machine.

1 Pick an instrument

not requested

MPE gives every sounding note its own MIDI channel, so a bend, a press or a slide belongs to one finger instead of to the whole keyboard — which is what a Seaboard, a LinnStrument or an Osmose needs to play at all. Bend and press reach the engine where its own written glides do, the rate and the gain; slide’s landing on the cutoff is this page’s convention, not the game’s — LBP3 has no MIDI to be faithful to. Instruments that leave their filter wide open will not answer it.

What this bench is for

Every slot at its base note plays each sample at ratio exactly 1.0 — they should sound like one instrument, and a timbre step between them means the splits or the pitch formula are wrong. The coloured bar on each key shows which slot it resolves to, so the key-split zones are visible while you play them. Every pitched sample carries a loop point in its smpl chunk and the mixer honours it, so a note’s length follows the note, not the sample divided by the playback rate.

The control. Switching engine to “browser resampler” plays the identical sample at the identical pitch through the browser’s own resampler. Clean there and dirty here means the fault is ours. That control is what proved an earlier distortion was the wrong source material — the 4-bit SFX-bank copies — rather than anything in this pipeline.
